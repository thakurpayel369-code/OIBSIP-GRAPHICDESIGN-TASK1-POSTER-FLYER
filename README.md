Mental Health Awareness Poster: It's Okay to Not Be Okay

A mental health awareness poster designed to spread positivity, encourage emotional well-being, and remind people that it is okay to express their feelings and ask for help. The design communicates an important message through a clean layout, minimal illustrations, and a calming colour palette.

---

📌 Poster Overview

- Project Name: Mental Health Awareness Poster
- Poster Title: It's Okay to Not Be Okay
- Category: Mental Health Awareness / Social Awareness
- Design Tool: Canva
- Designed By: Payel Thakur
- Visual Theme: Minimalist, calming, and emotional, with a combination of dark green, cream, black, and muted olive green.
- Purpose: To promote mental health awareness and encourage people to prioritize their emotional well-being.

---

📝 Text Content & Details

Main Heading

MENTAL HEALTH MATTERS

IT'S OKAY TO NOT BE OKAY

Supporting Message

Your mental health is just as important as your success.

Key Messages

- It's okay to feel. Recognize and accept your emotions without judging yourself.
- It's okay to ask for help. Reaching out for support is a sign of strength.
- It's okay to take your time. Give yourself the time and space you need to heal and grow.

Closing Message

Better days are still ahead.

---

🎨 Design Specifications

- Primary Colours: Dark Green, Cream, Black, and Muted Olive Green.
- Typography: A combination of bold sans-serif fonts for headings and elegant handwritten script fonts for emphasis.
- Layout: Vertical poster layout with a clear visual hierarchy.
- Illustrations: A woman with closed eyes, a stylized hairstyle, and minimal line-art elements.
- Graphic Elements:
  - Abstract dark green shapes at the top and bottom.
  - A cream-coloured background for readability.
  - Minimal icons representing the mind, heart, and self-care.
  - Handwritten typography to create an emotional and personal touch.
  - A small heart illustration near the closing message.

---

💡 Design Objective

The objective of this poster is to communicate the importance of mental health in a simple, relatable, and visually appealing way. The use of calming colours, minimal illustrations, and supportive messages helps create a comforting atmosphere while encouraging people to acknowledge their emotions and seek support when needed.

---

🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- Canva for poster design and layout.
- Typography pairing and text hierarchy.
- Colour coordination and contrast.
- Illustration placement and visual balance.
- Minimalist graphic design techniques.

---

📷 Poster Preview

The final poster features the message "It's Okay to Not Be Okay" alongside a calming illustration and supportive reminders about feelings, asking for help, and taking time for oneself.

---

🌿 Key Takeaway

Mental health matters just as much as physical health and professional success. Everyone deserves time, care, and support. Remember, it's okay to not be okay, and better days are still ahead.

Environmental Awareness Poster: Green Tomorrow

A promotional event poster designed for Green Steps Club to raise awareness about environmental protection and encourage people to take responsibility for creating a greener and more sustainable future.

---

📌 Poster Overview

- Organization Name: Green Steps Club
- Event Title: Green Tomorrow
- Event Type: Environmental Awareness Drive
- Tagline: One Planet. One Responsibility
- Event Date: November 2, 2026
- Day: Monday
- Event Time: 10:00 AM
- Venue: City Community Hall
- Designed By: Payel Thakur
- Design Tool: Canva
- Visual Theme: Nature-inspired, eco-friendly, clean, and modern design using different shades of green with a soft cream background.

---

📝 Text Content & Details

Header

GREEN STEPS CLUB PRESENTS

Main Heading

GREEN TOMORROW

Tagline

One Planet. One Responsibility

Event Highlight

ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS DRIVE

Event Details

- Date: 2 November 2026
- Time: 10:00 AM
- Venue: City Community Hall

Supporting Message

Small actions make a big impact.

Call to Action

BE PART OF THE CHANGE

Key Environmental Goals

- Cleaner Environment: Encourage eco-friendly habits and reduce pollution.
- Greener Communities: Promote sustainable practices and community participation.
- A Brighter Future: Protect natural resources for future generations.

---

🎨 Design Specifications

- Primary Colours: Dark Green, Olive Green, Leaf Green, and Cream.
- Typography: Bold brush-style fonts for the main heading, clean sans-serif fonts for event details, and handwritten script fonts for supporting messages.
- Layout: Vertical event poster with a balanced arrangement of text, illustrations, and event information.
- Main Visual: A globe representing Earth held carefully in human hands and surrounded by green leaves.
- Graphic Elements:
  - Green abstract shapes in the top-right corner.
  - Decorative botanical leaves in the top-right and bottom-left corners.
  - Brushstroke-style green backgrounds to highlight the event title and call to action.
  - Minimal icons for the date, time, and venue.
  - Environmental symbols representing sustainability and recycling.
  - A soft cream background to maintain readability and visual balance.

---

💡 Design Objective

The objective of this poster is to spread environmental awareness and motivate people to participate in sustainable activities. The globe surrounded by leaves and supported by human hands symbolizes our shared responsibility to protect the planet.

The poster uses nature-inspired colours, bold typography, and clear event details to create an attractive and informative design.

---

🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- Canva for poster creation and layout.
- Colour coordination using nature-inspired shades.
- Typography pairing and visual hierarchy.
- Image placement and composition.
- Decorative botanical elements and environmental icons.
- Contrast and spacing for improved readability.

---

📷 Poster Preview

The final design features the heading "Green Tomorrow," an environmental awareness drive announcement, event information, and a central illustration of Earth held in human hands surrounded by leaves.

The poster encourages individuals to take small steps toward a cleaner environment, greener communities, and a brighter future.

---

🌱 Key Takeaway

Protecting our planet starts with small actions. By working together and adopting sustainable habits, we can create a cleaner, greener, and healthier future.

One Planet. One Responsibility. Be Part of the Change.
